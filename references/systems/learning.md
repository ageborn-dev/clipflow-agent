# System 8 — Learning Summary

## Trigger
User says "what have you learned", "show patterns", "feedback loop",
"what's working", "learning summary"

---

- `memory/performance-log.md`
- `memory/competitor-scans.md`

```

{Formats outperforming — with data}
{Title patterns driving highest CTR}
{Any consistent patterns across top performers}

{Patterns from rejected ideas}
{Formats that consistently underperform}
{Anything to stop doing}

### Your timing model
Current best slot: {slot} — {confidence} ({N} data points)
Second best: {slot} — {confidence}
Status: {how many more videos until high confidence}

### Niche signals right now
{What competitor scans show is trending}
{Angles winning in your niche you haven't tried}

### What I'm adjusting
{Specific changes to how ClipFlow generates ideas}
{Timing weight changes based on performance data}
{Voice patterns being reinforced}
```
